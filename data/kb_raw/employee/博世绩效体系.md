# 博世绩效体系

## Performance 定义

**Performance** reflects how well someone is delivering on their current role, responsibilities, and expectations. It is about tangible results and current success.

```text
Performance = Deliverables + Competence + Behavior
```

---

## 绩效等级说明

| 等级 | 名称 | 定义 | 具体表现   |
| -- | ------------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 1  | Outstanding        | Performance is exceptional and consistently exceeds all expectations.                                      | - Systemic / organizational / process-wise impact<br>- Best one(s) in the team / cross teams<br>- Role model for others  |
| 2  | Exceed    | Performance consistently goes above and beyond the agreed-upon expectations.                               | - Willing to take initiative to contribute more than required<br>- Shows a high level of competence<br>- Proactively identifies opportunities for improvement<br>- Delivers high-quality work that adds notable value |
| 3  | Achieved    | Performance is solid and consistently meets all the requirements of the role.             | - Consistently meets requirements with periodic spikes up<br>- Achieves all key goals<br>- Reliable and dependable   |
| 4  | Partially Achieved | Performance inconsistently meets the core requirements of the role.              | - Meets some but not all goals<br>- Performance is inconsistent<br>- Work may require more revisions or supervision than expected<br>- Struggles with certain key skills or responsibilities       |
| 5  | Not Achieved   | Performance is consistently below expectations and fails to meet the fundamental requirements of the role. | - Significant gaps in skills, output, or behavior are evident<br>- Demonstrates a lack of necessary skills or effort<br>- Behavior may negatively impact the team    |

---

## 1. Outstanding

**Definition:**
Performance is exceptional and consistently exceeds all expectations.

**Indicators:**

* Systemic / organizational / process-wise impact
* Best one(s) in the team / cross teams
* Role model for others

---

## 2. Exceed

**Definition:**
Performance consistently goes above and beyond the agreed-upon expectations.

**Indicators:**

* Willing to take initiative to contribute more than required
* Shows a high level of competence
* Proactively identifies opportunities for improvement
* Delivers high-quality work that adds notable value

---

## 3. Achieved

**Definition:**
Performance is solid and consistently meets all the requirements of the role.

**Indicators:**

* Consistently meets requirements with periodic spikes up
* Achieves all key goals
* Reliable and dependable

---

## 4. Partially Achieved

**Definition:**
Performance inconsistently meets the core requirements of the role.

**Indicators:**

* Meets some but not all goals
* Performance is inconsistent
* Work may require more revisions or supervision than expected
* Struggles with certain key skills or responsibilities

---

## 5. Not Achieved

**Definition:**
Performance is consistently below expectations and fails to meet the fundamental requirements of the role.

**Indicators:**

* Significant gaps in skills, output, or behavior are evident
* Demonstrates a lack of necessary skills or effort
* Behavior may negatively impact the team
