## Our High Performance Culture in China  
我们的中国区高绩效文化  
### Collaboration to DELIVER  
协同共进  
- Proactively share information and resources to break down departmental silos.  
- When a project hits a bottleneck, immediately align relevant stakeholders to solve the problem rather than shifting blame or waiting.  
- Take ownership of shared goals, and proactively support upstream and downstream tasks while completing your own.  
- Promote and appreciate joint success, and win as a team.  
- 主动共享信息与资源，打破部门墙  
- 在项目遇到瓶颈时，第一时间拉通相关方共同解决问题，而非推诿或等待  
- 对团队共同目标负责，个人任务完成中主动支援上下游  
- 促进并珍视共同成功，团队制胜  
### Innovation to SHAPE  
创变未来  
- Never settle for existing processes or solutions—proactively ask, “Is there a better way?” Introduce new technologies and cross-disciplinary approaches early in the project.  
- Tolerate failure but demand rapid validation and fast iteration, and prove the value of innovation through business results.  
- 不满足于现有流程和方案，主动提出“有没有更好的做法”；在项目早期引入新技术和跨领域思路  
- 对失败有容忍度，但要求快速验证、快速迭代，用结果证明创新价值  
### Customer-centricity to GROW  
聚力共赢  
- Listen actively to customers/users, think from their perspectives, and uncover hidden needs and pain points.  
- Deliver end-to-end solutions and create value for customers/users.  
- Grow together with customers/users in both capabilities and business.  
- 主动聆听客户/用户的声音，站在他们的角度思考，识别与发现深层次的需求与痛点  
- 用一体化的能力，推动解决方案的落地，创造价值  
- 实现组织能力与业务的共同成长，与客户共赢  
### Commitment to WIN  
使命必达  
- Take on tough tasks in competitive projects without retreating.  
- Honor your commitments on QCD as promised, and be resilient when facing difficulties and proactively develop alternative solutions.  
- Make no excuses in retrospective meetings; instead, provide improvement plans and execute them at once.  
- Desire to grow with the company, and rotate based on business demand.  
- Define “winning” as outperforming competitors or achieving stretch goals.  
- 在竞争性项目中主动承担困难任务，不退缩  
- 对承诺的质量，成本和交付“说到做到”，即使遇到困难也坚忍不拔并主动寻找备选方案  
- 对失败复盘不找借口，而是输出改进计划并立即执行  
- 渴望与公司共成长，到业务最需要的地方去  
- 将“赢”定义为超越对手/达成挑战目标
