# T_Job Profile  
## Technical Position Definition & Career ladder  
| Job Title | Grade | Knowledge知识  | Innovation创新 | Impact影响 | Communication沟通 |
|----- -----|-------|----------------|----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Chief Expert<br>首席专家 | SL2   | Apply thought leadership and expertise in niche areas to shape company direction.<br>在细分领域运用思想领导力和专业知识，引导公司发展方向。 | Drive and facilitate global/regional innovation breakthroughs, resolve critical & complex problems by adopting or developing new methodologies.<br>推动并促成全球/区域创新突破，通过采用或开发新方法解决关键复杂问题。 | Global/regional accountability for organizational business strategy; as guru in own domains, spread unique knowledge by coaching/mentoring fellow experts.<br>对全球/区域业务战略负责；作为领域导师，通过辅导/指导同行专家传播独特知识。 | Communicate with global influence, represent company in industry forums, shape ecosystem discourse.<br>具备全球影响力沟通，代表公司参与行业论坛，引领产业话语权。 |
| Senior Expert<br>高级专家 | SL1   | Recognized industry - level expertise, shape company technical direction.<br>具备行业级专业知识，引导公司技术方向。 | Pioneer new domains, create breakthrough solutions.<br>开拓新领域，创造突破性解决方案。 | Impact company - wide strategy, enable long - term competitiveness.<br>影响公司整体战略，提升长期竞争力。 | Strategic communicator with executives, influences decision - making.<br>面向高层进行战略沟通，影响决策。 |
| Advanced Expert<br>专家工程师 | G9   | Broad and deep expertise, integrates knowledge across domains.<br>兼具深度与广度，能整合跨领域知识。 | Drive technical innovation, establish methodologies and best practices.<br>推动技术创新，建立方法论和最佳实践。 | Influence departmental/organizational results, ensure scalability and sustainability.<br>影响部门/组织成果，确保可扩展性与可持续性。 | Persuasive communicator, aligns diverse stakeholders.<br>善于说服沟通，协调多方利益相关者。 |
| Senior Engineer<br>高级工程师 | G8    | Possess advanced expertise and apply cross - disciplinary knowledge.<br>具备高级专业知识，能跨领域应用。 | Develop and implement new approaches within projects.<br>在项目中提出并实施新方法。 | Impact project delivery, ensure technical quality.<br>对项目交付和技术质量产生影响。 | Clearly articulate complex concepts, mentor juniors.<br>能清晰表达复杂概念，指导初级成员。 |
| Engineer<br>工程师 | G7    | Demonstrate solid technical knowledge, independently solve routine problems.<br>具备扎实专业知识，能独立解决常规问题。 | Suggest improvements within defined frameworks.<br>在既定框架内提出优化建议。 | Influence small team or project tasks.<br>影响小团队或项目任务。 | Communicates effectively with peers and stakeholders on issues.<br>与同事和相关方有效沟通问题。 |
| Junior Engineer<br>初级工程师 | G6    | Apply basic theoretical knowledge, execute tasks with supervision.<br>应用基础理论知识，在指导下完成任务。 | Follow established procedures, limited scope for adaptation.<br>遵循既定流程，改进空间有限。 | Impact limited to the quality and timeliness of own deliverables.<br>影响主要体现在个人交付的质量和及时性。 | Communicates basic information within team.<br>在团队内部进行基本沟通。 |
