# Self-reflection / Preparation Dialog Partner
**© Robert Bosch GmbH | C/HRP | 08/2023**
Development Dialog focuses on your development of the associate and is intended to encourage reflection, discovery, motivation, and growth as well as to give new impulses. 
Use this self-reflection to prepare for the dialog. 
Come prepared and bring your ideas and perspectives to the dialog. 
*Optional: For further impulses, you can also take a look at MyDevelopment Store. There you will find an overview of various topics and offers for actively shaping careers at Bosch.*
---
### Purpose of the dialog
Define a goal for your dialog. Which topics should be focused?
### Reflection & Feedback (strengths, successes)
The following questions can help you finding out more about your partner:
- What are the experiences and successes you are proud of?
- What is your definition of success?
- What is the red thread in your development journey so far?
- What feedback have you gathered? What do you want to keep doing and what do you want to rethink?
### Personal expectations (motivation, wishes, interests)
Remember that goals can be “big or small”. Keep in mind different growth options like lateral, horizontal, vertical movements as well as growing in place. Try to listen and understand the person’s strengths and interests and help to align them with the needs of the company.
- How do your interests align with company goals?
- Which roles do you want to take over? Where do you want to contribute more?
- How do your business goals fit with your private goals?
- Your perfect job – what would that be?
- What would have happened differently if …?
### Future Goals & Sweet Spot
Ask the dialog partner the following questions:
- What are requirements and needs for our future business success?
- Which skills will be essential for your job in the future?
- Where do your interests and strengths overlap with the company‘s requirements now and in the future?
**You might offer your dialog partner the following:**
- Your goal might be … (based on your strengths and motivation I heard)
- Go out and talk to colleagues who carry out similar/same tasks
- Are there any role models who you can talk to?
- Expand your network also to find out more about the organizational environment (→ use your network)
- Get support from a Mentor, from a People Coach
### Take Aways & Next Steps
As a dialog partner, offer your expertise, network, support…
- Based on what I understand about your motivation and goals, you could think about the following …
- I will contribute… I will support in the following way… Your next steps could be…
- Have you heard of …? Is it interesting for you?
- I know … (person) who you can talk to about it. Are you interested?
### What’s next?
Help your dialog partner to define the next steps towards his/her goals.
- What could be a very first step? When are you going to start?
- Who can support you? Who do you need to talk to?
- What do you need to learn?
---
Make sure you are well prepared. This is the best way to show appreciation and achieve the goal of the dialog.
### Are you ready for the Development Dialog?
Reflect on the quality of your conversation at the end of the dialog, share your reflections with each other.
Have you had a good conversation?
Not a perfect result? This can happen. 
Discuss, what you would like to keep / start / stop doing before and during the next conversation. Improve your conversations step-by-step.
