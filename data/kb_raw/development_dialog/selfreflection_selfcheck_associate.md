# Self-reflection / Preparation: Associate
**© Robert Bosch GmbH | C/HRP | 08/2023**
Development Dialog focuses on your development of the associate and is intended to encourage reflection, discovery, motivation, and growth as well as to give new impulses. 
Use this self-reflection to prepare for the dialog. 
Come prepared and bring your ideas and perspectives to the dialog. 
*Optional: For further impulses, you can also take a look at MyDevelopment Store. There you will find an overview of various topics and offers for actively shaping careers at Bosch.*
---
### Purpose of the dialog
To get the most out of your dialog, think about the following in advance: What is the goal of the conversation for you? Which topics should be focused?
### Reflection & Feedback (strengths, successes)
Use the following questions as food for thought for your conversation:
- What are the experiences and successes I am proud of?
- In which moments did/do I feel most valuabe?
- What energizes me?
- What am I looking forward to in the near future?
### Personal expectations (motivation, wishes, interests)
Find out more about where you see yourself in the future. Remember that the goals can be “big or small”. Consider different growth options like lateral, horizontal, vertical movements as well as growing in place. The broader you think, the more opportunities you see.
- What kind of footprint do I wish to make?
- What do I want to be known for?
- Which roles do I want to take over? Where do I want to contribute more?
- How can I align my interests with company goals?
### Future Goals & Sweet Spot
Ask yourself the following questions:
- What are requirements and needs for our future business success?
- Which skills will be essential for my job in the future?
- What else should I be doing / what should I do less of?
- Where do my interests and strengths overlap with the company‘s requirements now and in the future?
**What can you do:**
- Go out and talk to colleagues who carry out similar/same tasks.
- Are there any role models you can talk to?
- Expand your network also to find out more about the organizational environment.
- Get support from a Mentor or from a People Coach.
### Take Aways & Next Steps
Use the following questions and tips to commit more to your goals:
- What could be a very first step? What do I need to do to come one step closer to my goals?
- When do I want to have reached my goal?
- Who can support me? Who do I need to talk to?
- What do I need to learn?
### What’s next?
Use your development plan:
- … to get to know your aspirations and what motivates you in your work
- … to know where you stand and that you are able to communicate if you need individual support
- … as preparation for dialogs
- … to work out and show your contribution
- … to talk to people who could support you in your development (e.g. mentor, leader, coach, HR)
- … to keep on working on your own
- … update your profile in HR Global, so your skills and experiences are visible, and you can be identified
