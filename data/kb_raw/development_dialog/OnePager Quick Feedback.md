Good feedback is the kind of attention, which makes people stronger. 
It is not about giving only positive feedback, It is about creating the right frame to exchange more honest feedback. 
We just must do it with care and respect. Both positive and constructive feedback improve relationship, increase trust and enable high individual and team performance. 
---
## What is a good Feedback?
### When giving feedback:
- think „What could support?“, not how to win/express emotions, etc.
- comment behaviours, not people or outcomes
- tell what people do well, don’t expect them to know, they often don’t
- give short clear simple messages, they can be processed easier
- remember, that sharing is caring! Addressing feedback is a must. Just create the right frame for it (see above)
- about development opportunities, speak about overused strengths, underused strengths, applying available strengths in the new context. Address weaknesses only if they stand in the person’s way to reach the goal.
### When asking for feedback:
- ask for things, that work good – you want to keep them and build on them
- ask for things, that might help you reach your goals / build even better relationships -- you want to understand, which skills you could develop to reach more / feel better
- be thankful for the time and attention, invested by the feedback giver
- take the feedback as a gift
- do your best to understand the feedback and decide, what you would like to do with it
---
### Core Principles of Feedback:
- **Cherish strengths**
- **Explore together**
- **Talk often**

## Quick Feedback Functionality
Use this simple HR Global functionality to learn from feedback and to support others with your feedback. Good feedback helps you clarify expectations, develop yourself and strengthen relationships with your colleagues.
- Give/ask for feedback from anybody in the company
- Have full control over the feedbacks – keep it or delete it
- Collect multiperspective feedback in one tool (HR Global), use it for reflection on your development and/or for preparation for your dialogues
