## Key HR Process and activities throughout the year
（全年关键HR流程与活动）
| 月份 | 关键HR流程与活动 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Jan** | Jan 1 Admission to TP (1月1日加入人才池)<br>Manager and associates prepare & conduct GPD planning (经理和员工准备并开展GPD-计划)<br>Leader’s Salon (领导者沙龙) |
| **Feb - Mar** | Manager and associates prepare & conduct GPD planning (继续准备并开展GPD-计划) |

| **Jun** | Salary planning + approval (薪酬规划与审批)<br>ASR feedback to associates (向员工提供年度薪酬回顾反馈)<br>TPx (self) nomination (人才池自荐) |
| **Jul - Aug** | TPx Exchange meetings for validation & decisions (人才池交流会以进行验证和决策)<br>July 1 Admission to TP (7月1日加入人才池)<br>ASR feedback to associates (向员工提供年度薪酬回顾反馈)<br>Leader’s Salon (领导者沙龙)<br>TP members’ activities (人才池成员活动) |
| **Sep** | TAR Preparation (人才与员工回顾准备)<br>TCL Planning & evaluation (目标薪酬级别规划与评估) |
| **Oct** | People Conference (人员会议)<br>TP0/1/2, TAR SL1/SL2<br>TCL SL1/SL2 validation (if needed) (TCL SL1/SL2验证，如需)<br>Feedback to associates after People Conference (人员会议后向员工提供反馈)<br>Leader’s Salon (领导者沙龙)<br>Diversity week (多元化周) |
| **Nov - Dec** | Manager invite associates and prepare for GPD review (经理邀请员工并准备GPD-回顾)<br>Manager defines and reviews key positions with HR BP (经理与HRBP一起定义和回顾关键岗位)<br>Manager discusses possible successors for key position with HR BP in People Conference (经理在人员会议上与HRBP讨论关键岗位的可能继任者) |

### Talent Development Dialogs (人才发展对话)
- **CDD (Career Development Dialogue)**: CDD is held at any time of a year, at associate’s own request or at the request of his/her managers or HRBP. (职业发展对话可在一年中的任何时间举行，基于员工自己的请求或其经理或HRBP的请求。)
- **CAD (Career Advancement Dialogue)**: CAD is held within 6 months of associates’ TP nomination. (职业晋升对话在员工被提名入人才池后的6个月内进行。)
