## 1. ESSENTIALS ON LEADERSHIP AT BOSCH (博世领导力精要)
### Your Inner Management Team is supporting you!
You are not alone! Your super “inner management team” is consisted of four major roles: Company representative, Content Expert, Team Coach, and a personally involved individual. 
- **Company representative**: Company’s vision, mission and strategy; Result/key task-oriented; Company’s interests and purpose; Cost Effectiveness; Business Context Analysis
- **Team Coach**: Show appreciation; Support collaboration within and out of teams; Develop team strengths; Motivate associates; Develop team member basing on individual personality and needs; Help team collaborate with multi-stakeholders
- **Content Expert**: Conduct dialogues on feasibility, process, plan, resources needed, capabilities, quality, efficiency; How to optimize and continuously improve; How to solve problem; How to be successful
- **Personally involved individual**: Express own emotion, feeling, attitude, subjective perspectives
### Framework of Different Concepts
- **Our vision statement**: Invented for Life
- **Mission statement**: We are Bosch
- **Leadership principle**: We Lead Bosch
### LEAD WORK WIN #LikeABosch
- **Our “Why”**: Invented for life is purpose and business as well
- **Our “What”**: Accelerate growth; Deliver performance; Develop Bosch
- **Our “How”**:
  - **LEAD #LikeABosch**: Empower high performance; Grow diverse talent; Drive sustainability and digital business
  - **WORK #LikeABosch**: Excite your customers; Foster trust and deliver results; Learn fast and collaborate
  - **WIN #LikeABosch**: Outperform competition; Be persistent and have guts; Celebrate success
### Bosch Leadership Requirement & Competence Model
As a role model, We Lead Bosch principle and Leadership Requirement are Bosch’s expectations for leaders’ behaviors. As a people manager, competence model is the foundation for new hires, performance reviews and assessments of potential.
### Work #LikeABosch - We grow, enjoy and inspire each other
- **Grow (成长，定义不凡)**: Reinvent yourself, Discover new directions.
- **Enjoy (生活，定格乐活)**: Be yourself, Balance your life, Celebrate success.
- **Inspire (灵感，慧启世界)**: Shape tomorrow.

## 2. NEW MANAGERS’ INTEGRATION PLAN (新经理融入计划)
### Suggested to-dos per the onboarding in 0-2 years
| Topics | Timing & Duration | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Onboarding/Transition Dialogue with supervisor | 0 - 1 month, 2 hours | • Gain necessary awareness for taking on the position<br>• clarify mutual expectations with the new associate<br>• Get important information about the current team |
| Goal Setting with the team/individuals | 0 - 1 year, 0.5 - 1 day | • To create the basis for a trusting, open work relationship<br>• (Re-)align the team goal setting or revisit based on the new business needs<br>• Steer the strategic directions if needed |
| Transition Workshop with the team | 2 - 6 months, 0.5 day | • openly discuss mutual expectations between the leader and the team members<br>• Understand the development of team’s assets/strengths and needed improvement/relevant issues<br>• Help the team members to get to know the leader holistically<br>• set up the future orientation and show appreciation |

## 3. YEARLY CALENDAR OF PEOPLE-DEVELOPMENT TOPICS (人才发展话题年历表)
### Key HR Process and activities throughout the year
- **Jan**: Jan 1 Salary adjustment; TPx (self) nomination; Manager and associates prepare & conduct Contribution Dialog – planning part; Leader’s Salon
- **Feb-May**: Adjust Priorities along the year; Give and ask for feedback; TPx Exchange meetings for validation & decisions; RBCN People Week
- **Jun**: Salary planning + approval; Leader’s Salon
- **Jul**: July 1 Admission to TP; ASR feedback to associates; TP members’ activities
- **Aug**: TAR Preparation; Leader’s Salon
- **Sep**: TCL Planning & evaluation; Diversity week
- **Oct**: People Conference; TP0/1/2, TAR SL1/SL2; TCL SL1/SL2 validation (if needed); Feedback to associates after People Conference
- **Nov**: Manager defines and reviews key positions with HR BP; Manager invite associates prepare and conduct Contribution dialog – review part
- **Dec**: Manager discusses possible successors for key position with HR BP in People Conference
- **Anytime**: Development Dialog; Quick Feedback
### Talent Pool (TP) Process Overview
- **Talent**: Self nomination via HR Global → Inform Leader → Collect feedback → Forward to HR BP via HR Global → Reflect on motivation and goals → Discuss with HR BP → Provide input for People Conference → Receive feedback from leader → Participate in Talent Development Program
- **Leader**: Nomination via HR Global → Information to talent & HR BP → Support talent to find feedback providers → Reflect feedback with talent → Share input on talent with HR BP prior to PeCo → Pitch talent in PeCo → Share feedback & result with talent → Support talent on development journey → Receive/hand over Talent Pool admission letter
### People Dialogs (Effective Oct. 1 2024)
- **Quick Feedback**: An optional functionality to ask or give real-time feedback.
- **Development Dialog**: An optional Format to discuss individual development (replaces CDD, CAD, IDD, Get-to-know-conversation).
- **Contribution Dialog**: A yearly dialog about successes, collaboration, motivation and development (replaces GPD, IDD and YEC).
### Contribution Dialog
- **Changes compared with GPD**:
  - *Process*: GPD (7 routing steps, 13 forms) → Contribution Dialog (3 routing steps, 1 form in HR Global, Real-time feedback function*).
  - *Dialog Content*: Year planning (Motivation*, Goal setting w/ Contribution Goals*: Results | Collaboration | Learning, Learning & Development*, Development measures, Cptm & Compliance Checkbox) & Year review (Goal achievement, Positive Highlights & Successes*, Appreciation & Collaboration*, Other topics).
- **Preparation sections**: Motivation; Positive Highlights & Successes; Appreciation & Collaboration; Learning & Development; Other topics; Development Measures.
### Development Dialog
- **Changes compared to CDD and CAD**:
  - *Process*: CDD/CAD (4 forms, Standard route map: Target Manager & HRBP) → Development Dialog (1 form, Initiation depending on use case, Standard route map: Associate* & HRBP, Dialog Partners can be added for acknowledgment*).
  - *Dialog Content*: Dialog Purpose*, Self-Reflection & Motivation*, Feedback*, Future Requirements & Sweet Spot*, Agreements & Next Steps, Development Measures. No Checkbox in the form (to be filled in Profile in HR Global*), Possible to add attachments*.
### Quick Feedback
- Give/ask for feedback from anybody in the company.
- Have full control over the feedbacks: delete, keep, make visible to your leader.
- Collect multiperspective feedback in one tool (HR Global).

## 4. ASSOCIATES MANAGEMENT (员工管理)
*Overview: Along with the journey of associates from onboarding to offboard/transferring, the basic key points in the processes are listed to support managers grasp the essence in the managerial activities.*
### Hiring Process
1. **Requisition**: Hiring Manager identifies staffing needs and gets "Personnel Requisition" approved. Recruiter creates job requisition in TalentHub.
2. **Pre-selection**: Recruiter publishes/advertises the position. Hiring Manager reviews candidates. Recruiter reviews and pre-selects applications to create shortlists.
3. **Interview**: Hiring Team conducts interviews, reviews candidates, fills in scorecard, and compares candidates for evaluation.
4. **Offer**: Hiring Team makes final decision. Recruiter creates offer proposal in TalentHub, gets approval, gets signatures, and extends the offer to the candidate.
5. **Hire**: Recruiter receives signed offer, informs hiring team, rejects/reassigns other candidates, and prepares for onboarding.
### Onboarding – Roles of managers and buddies
- **Manager's responsibilities**: Help new employee go through the first 6 months period and adapt to new tasks/position quickly. Assist with communication, culture transfer, on-site visits, etc.
- **Buddy's responsibilities**: Usually a departmental colleague who helps new employee be familiar with the work environment. Goals include: Advice/support, Fast integration, Informal information and support for team building, Broaden the horizon/exchange of experience.
### Probation Review
- **Timeline**: 6 months (usually 3-year contract). HRS sends notification one month in advance.
- **Participants**: Employee, Direct manager.
- **Content**: Performance review, Competence evaluation, Probation assessment result, Training needs.
- **Process**: Participate in the review meeting and send out signed probation evaluation to HR contract person and HRBP before the probation end date. (If performance concerns exist, contact HR BP at the early stage).
### Labor Contract Renewal
HRS sends email notification 2 months in advance.
- If renew: Manager sends confirmation to HRS contact person and HRBP.
- If not renew: Contact HRBP for managing out process as early as possible.
### Internal Transfer Process
- **Requisition**: Publish all approved positions in TalentHub (SL1, SL2, SL3). Internal candidate pool preferred.
- **Pre-selection**: Indirect associates should have stayed on current task for 24 months before moving.
- **Interview**: Structured interview must be conducted by recruiting HRL and Hiring Management. Supervisors are not permitted to deny or hinder job interviews.
- **Offer**: Offer letter & Internal Transfer Letter confirmation from TalentHub.
- **Hire**: Transfer should occur within three months at the latest. Transfer date should be the 1st day of each month aligned by both sides.
### Termination Process
- Associate resignation shall be submitted via online system (e.g. i-Termination).
- Associates during probation shall notify 3 days prior; Regular associates below SL1 shall notify at least 30 days prior.
- Accumulated leave shall not offset the notice period in principle.
- Manager needs to arrange Exit Interview, understand reasons, take retention measures for key employee if necessary.
- Manager needs to arrange Handovers, ensure employee returns company assets and finishes online termination process.

## 5. ASSOCIATES DEVELOPMENT AND LEARNING (员工发展与学习)
### Me@Mybest
I am @ my best: when I am doing work I am good at, when the work energizes me, and when it is aligned with business goals. Manager's responsibility is to support associates to find their strength and develop them based on their strengths.
### 5 clues to find the talents of your associates
1. **Needs (无限向往)**: Where are you naturally drawn to?
2. **Flow (行云流水)**: What goes automatically, doesn't feel like work?
3. **(Surprising) outstanding achievements (胜人一筹)**: When have you asked yourself, "How did I do that?"
4. **Satisfaction (乐在其中)**: What gives you the kick so that you want to do it again and again?
5. **Quick perception (一学就会)**: What do you understand quickly?
### 70-20-10 Learning Model Use Case
- **70% On-the-job learning**: Shadowing, Job rotation, Task force, Temporary projects, New tasks outside main responsibilities, Deputy/proxy, etc.
- **20% Learning from others**: Feedback from others, Bosch Club, Mentoring offers, Networking, Feedback loops, Professional guilds, WOL, etc.
- **10% Formal learning**: my Transform, Competence Management@Bosch, eUniversities, Learning Videos, Playlists & Learning Paths, Web-based Training, etc. (One-stop platform: CHINA LEARNING RESOURCES, BTC Hotline: 021-2218 8666)

## 6. FEEDBACK AND MOTIVATION (反馈与激励)
### Gain Feedback as a leader
Every leader at every level is required to seek feedback on their leadership behavior in a structural approach at least every two years.
- **FOCUSED Feedback**: Leadership Feedback 360° (on-site & remote)
- **BROAD Feedback**: Feedback Dart (on-site & remote)
- **MULTI-PERSPECTIVE Feedback**: Feedback Express Workshop (on-site & remote)
### Providing Forward-looking Feedback with AID Model
- **ACTION**: Share your perception without judging. Describe what you have observed. Avoid assumptions and interpretations. (What behaviors/actions were observed?)
- **IMPACT**: Share the impact of the behavior. Explain how it influenced you or others. Focus on both positive/negative consequences. (What influence the action had on success/result?)
- **DO differently**: Describe your wish for different behavior, or the behavior which should be repeated in future. Give ideas on how the person could further develop. (Which approach could lead to a better result still?)
### Motivate your associates with total rewards
Total Rewards @ Bosch China. As each individual has his/her own motivations, you can consult with your HRBP in addition to the framework.

## 7. SHAPE HIGH PERFORMANCE CULTURE AND ASSOCIATES EXPERIENCE TOGETHER (共塑高绩效文化与卓越员工体验)
### Performance driver: High-Performance Culture Pyramid
Source: Lencioni’s Five Behaviors Framework. To tackle the 5 dysfunctions of a team in the pyramid will guide your team towards a high-performance culture.
### Experience Driver: 5 KPIs
Five KPIs are standardized indicators that enable comparisons across organizations. "Bosch Pulse Check(BPC)" is applied to monitor the status.
1. **Engagement**: If people are engaged, they will be more motivated to meet or exceed goals.
2. **Experience**: If experience exceed expectations, everyone wins.
3. **Intend to stay**: Get a clear indicator of how long people would currently like to stay with the company.
4. **Inclusion**: Get a clear indicator of how included people feel.
5. **Wellbeing**: A high well-being score indicates employees' positive self-perception and trustworthy work relationships.
