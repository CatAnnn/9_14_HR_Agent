## 1. Object, aim and objective
This Central Directive (CD) regulates the application of different associate development processes and instruments at Bosch. The goals of associate development include strengthening Bosch as an attractive employer, utilizing associate competencies and potentials, and supporting associates in achieving their individual development and career goals. The aim of this CD is to ensure a minimum standard of quality in associate development worldwide at Bosch.
## 2. Field of application
This C-Directive is binding for the Bosch Corporate Group. For BEGE, which do not belong to the Bosch Corporate Group, it is recommended that they adopt the content of this C-Directive by their own decision.
Deviation from the provisions of this CD must be approved by the GPO as Governance Owner.
Leading language of this guideline is German.
This C-Directive replaces the CD 02606 issued 2.0 dated October 10th, 2020.
## 3. Definitions and abbreviations
In addition to the terms and abbreviations defined in RBGF 100, 101, 103, the following terms are defined:
- **CPE**: Country Policy and Process Expert
- **e-file**: Electronic personnel file
- **ETP**: Executive Talent Pool
- **GPO**: Global Policy and Process Owner
- **HR**: Function Human Resources
- **HR BP**: Human Resources Business Partner
- **HR Global**: IT-Tool for mapping of HR processes
- **Manager**: Manager with target and/or disciplinary responsibility, see RBGF101
- **SL**: Salary Level
- **SPL**: Succession Planning
- **TAR**: Talent & Associate Review
- **TP**: Talent Pool
## 4. Determinations
### 4.1 Roles
The execution dates for the individual processes can be found in the HR calendar (see attachments). (ND)
- **GPO**: Responsible for implementation, execution and monitoring of the respective HR processes and policies for the Bosch Group worldwide. GPO acts on behalf of C/HR who is overall accountable. GPO ensures necessary alignment with and approval by C/HR and Center of Expertise (CoE = C/HRP).
- **CPE**: Responsible for implementation of the respective HR processes at country level, e.g. alignment with national legal requirements. CPE acts on behalf of C/HRR who is overall accountable. CPE ensures necessary alignment with and approval by C/HRR.
- Policy and process responsibility thus lie with the respective GPO worldwide and at the country level with the respective CPE. (ND)
- If the adherence to regulations is not possible, or if local legal requirements speak against it, the CPE must define an exception together with the respective GPO. (DI)
- Responsible for the implementation, support and monitoring of the individual processes and formats is the responsible HR BP. (ND)

- **R**: Is Responsible
- **A**: Has to Approve
- **S**: Has to Support
- **I**: To be Informed
- **C**: To be Consulted, has veto power

### 4.3 The Bosch Competence Model (ND)
The Bosch Competence Model is the basis for associate development. It consists of four competence fields (entrepreneurial, leadership, social and professional competences), which are assigned two competence subsets each. (ND)
### 4.4 HR Global (ND)
If HR Global is rolled out, the use of HR Global is mandatory for the conduction of the processes described in this regulation, as far as compatible with collective agreements and legal regulations. This applies for all associate groups, except the respective process is not applied to a certain group of associates.
Forms are archived in HR Global upon completion and need to be stored in electronic personnel file according to country-specific regulations.
If HR Global is not available, the global standard forms of Contribution Dialog and Development Dialog must be used.
### 4.5 Contribution Dialog
The Contribution Dialog is a personal conversation about individual contribution, development, planning and achievement of tasks/projects, as well as, a performance evaluation if applicable. (ND)
- The Contribution Dialog is a yearly dialog between associate and target manager (ND). The target manager is responsible for the conduction of the Contribution Dialog. (ND) With mutual agreement, the disciplinary manager can take over the conduction of the dialog as well as additional dialog partners can participate. (DI)
- The target manager ensures the appropriate involvement and contribution to goal setting and performance evaluation of other relevant organizational roles. The participation of additional dialog partners in the Contribution Dialogue is only possible at the request or with the consent of the associate (e.g. see CD 02500). (ND)
- The Contribution Dialog is mandatory for associates starting from SL1 (or a comparable worldwide level). (ND) Additional groups of associates can be included. (DI)
- The respective HR BP is responsible for controlling the conduction of the Contribution Dialog. (DI)
### 4.6 Development Dialog
The Development Dialog is a dialog about personal and professional development goals of an associate with selected dialog partners. In general, associates choose their dialog partners themselves. (DI)
- The Development Dialog has different use cases and can be initiated at any time by the associate, manager or the HR BP. The goal and focus of the dialog are depending on the use case and therefore have to be determined and communicated by the initiator. (ND)
- The associate is responsible for the Development Dialog. (ND)
- The form is archived once all participants have acknowledged the form.
- In the case of admissions to Talent Pool, a Development Dialog must be conducted and documented. (ND) In this case, the responsible HR BP invites the associate and managers. (DI) In addition to the associate and the target manager, the participants include the next higher-level manager and, if different, the HR BP responsible for the target salary level (e.g. Executive HR BP for Development Dialogs towards SL3 in TP3). (DI)
- In justified cases, the conduction of a Development Dialog can be waived with the agreement of all parties (e.g., if a Development Dialog to discuss and establish individual development goals took place 6 months prior to the admission to the Talent Pool). (ND)
- The respective HR BP is responsible for controlling of the conduction of the Development Dialog. (DI)
### 4.7 Quick Feedback
Quick Feedback is an optional feature in HR Global that provides digital support for requesting and giving feedback. Giving, accepting, and sharing feedback is voluntary and can be done at any time. (ND)
### 4.8 Talent & Associate Review (TAR)
The TAR is a discussion between HR and the respective target manager, in which an assessment of the associates’ personal and professional development potential takes place. (ND)
- The TAR takes place once a year. It is to be conducted for associates from SL1 level or a comparable global level and upwards, as well as for associate groups below SL1 who may be nominated for the Talent Pool (e.g., from EG15 for BaWü). Additional associate groups may be included. (DI)
- The main component of the TAR is the People Conference. The People Conference is a discussion between HR, managers, and an extended leadership team, as well as potentially other relevant participants. (ND)
- In preparation of the People Conference, managers define for which associates a discussion is beneficial. The manager keeps an eye on all his associates. (DI)
- The respective HR BP is responsible for conducting the TAR and People Conference. (ND)
- TAR documents are to be deleted in accordance with national data protection legislation. In HR Global, the implementation is centralized per country, while in all other systems, this must be done manually by the target manager or the HR BP. (ND)
### 4.9 Succession Planning (SPL)
The aim of Succession planning is the definition and staffing of key positions. (ND)
- The identification of (high) potential successors is an ongoing process and should be particularly discussed in relation to staffing of key positions, as part of the yearly People Conferences. (DI)
- Individual development plans must be described and adjusted as needed as part of succession planning. (DI)
- The respective target manager, is responsible for succession planning and supporting the targeted development of identified (high) potential successors in coordination with HR. (ND)
- The respective HR BP is responsible for controlling the conduction of succession planning for key positions. (ND)
### 4.10 Talent Pool
The Talent Pool is an instrument for the development of associates, who show outstanding potential for specialist and management roles. (ND)
- For each target salary level from SL1-SL5 a Talent Pool must be offered. (ND)
- The membership in the Talent Pool is limited to three years and ends automatically after this time. It is still possible to take on a task at the next higher salary level even after the active Talent Pool membership has ended. If a Talent Pool member achieves the next higher salary level before three years are over, the membership in the Talent Pool ends with the date of the promotion. (ND)
- Nominations for a Talent Pool are based on potential, not on positions. Associates are nominated to the Talent Pool by their manager, HR BP or they can nominate themselves (self-nomination). (ND)
- Aim of the Executive Talent Pool (ETP) is the fast and early promotion of associates with recognizable potential for executive positions. The ETP supports the members in reaching the next two salary levels quickly. The Membership ends upon achieving this goal, but no later than after six years. (ND)
- Responsible for the admission and follow-up of the Talent Pool process is the respective HR BP. (DI) Admissions to a Talent Pool are to be ensured on a regular basis, generally twice a year. (DI)
*Further details on Succession Planning (SPL) can be found in MyHR.*
The decision on the admission to the respective Talent Pool is made according to the table below. In justified exceptional cases, the right to decide can be transferred. This has to be documented in a comprehensible manner. (DI)
| Current Salary Level | Talent Pool | Target | Decision on admission to the Talent Pool by… |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SL4 | Talent Pool 5 | SL5 | GBM resp. G |
| SL3 | Talent Pool 4 | SL4 | resp. G |
| SL2 | Talent Pool 3 | SL3 | BV |
| SL1 | Talent Pool 2 | SL2 | BV-1 resp. Management |
| Upper tariff range | Talent Pool 1 | SL1 | |
| SL2 | Executive Talent Pool 4 | SL4 | resp. G |
| SL1 | Executive Talent Pool 3 | SL3 | BV |
*\*For M functions, the sector board takes the place of the BV.*
- After admission to the Talent Pool, participation in respective defined measures (Talent Development Program) is mandatory, if available for the respective Talent Pool. (ND)
- Participation is not a precondition for promotion. (ND)
### 4.11 Career Elements
In order to be successful in an increasingly complex, diverse and global environment, specialists and managers at Bosch should have experience in cross-functional and international tasks as well as change perspectives.
It is of no relevance whether the experiences have been gained at Bosch or at another employer. (ND)
Next to personal and professional ability, career elements define experiences that are taken into account for a promotion decision to the next SL level (see attachment). (DI)
